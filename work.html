CCTV Shoplifting Prototype

Problem: Retailers lacked real‑time, automated detection of suspicious behaviour in stores.

Approach: Built a Python prototype using YOLOv8 & DeepSORT for object detection and tracking. Implemented skeleton‑sequence LSTM to classify shoplifting risk and surface alerts through a Streamlit interface.

Results: Demonstrated accurate detection of suspicious actions in sample videos, providing a foundation for potential retail‑security product.

Stack: Python, PyTorch, YOLOv8, DeepSORT, LSTM, Streamlit.
