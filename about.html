About Me

Get to know the person behind Karanam Sites.

Hello! I’m Raghavendra Karanam, a data‑driven engineer and problem solver based in Delray Beach, FL. I hold a Master’s degree in Data Science & Analytics from Florida Atlantic University (GPA 3.87). My passion lies in blending web development with analytics to build solutions that are reliable, secure and measurable.

With experience ranging from volunteer web projects for non‑profits to building ETL pipelines on AWS, I bring a unique perspective that bridges design, engineering and data. Whether it’s launching a fast site, optimising performance or developing dashboards, I treat every project as an opportunity to deliver lasting value.

When I’m not coding, I enjoy exploring Florida’s coast, reading about machine learning advances and contributing to open‑source projects. I believe in continuous learning and sharing knowledge with the community.

Values

  • Performance: Fast sites create happy users.
  • Reliability: Solid engineering & secure practices come first.
  • Measurement: Decisions should be driven by data.
  • Clarity: Communication and documentation matter as much as code.
